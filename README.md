
# Hema Rawat – Professional Portfolio

This repository documents my AI-assisted professional portfolio project. The portfolio presents my experience, education, skills, and business analytics projects in a clear and recruiter-friendly format.

## Portfolio Purpose

The portfolio was created for job applications, internships, career fairs, and professional networking. It presents a balanced career story connecting people, processes, data reporting, and business analytics.

## Portfolio Sections

* About
* Projects
* Experience
* Education
* Skills
* Contact
* Downloadable résumé
* Mobile-friendly quick-links page

## Design Direction

The portfolio uses navy blue, teal, cream/white, and a small gold accent. The “HR” initials serve as the professional monogram instead of a personal photograph.

## AI-Assisted Design Process

I used ChatGPT and Google Gemini to explore portfolio structure, content organization, branding, navigation, and mobile design. After comparing their recommendations, I selected and combined the ideas that best supported my goals and target audience.

## Key Improvements

* Clear and consistent navigation
* Skills organized into easy-to-scan categories
* Project descriptions showing tools and business value
* Strong buttons for projects, résumé, LinkedIn, and email
* Improved spacing, readability, and mobile responsiveness
* Separate mobile quick-links page
* Consistent professional branding
